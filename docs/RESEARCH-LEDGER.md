## How to use this ledger

Ranking is an editorial judgment of apparent usefulness to the c. 1492 atlas and
its internal c. 1550 extension: direct spatial/chronological evidence comes first,
then interpretive synthesis, then conservation methods and data-release watches.
Rank is **not** a confidence score. A lower-ranked source may be the best fit for
a particular feature. No numerical precision is implied by the ordering.

Statuses: `extract` = recover page/figure-level evidence before use;
`reconcile` = compare with existing project evidence;
`verify` = resolve a material provenance/identity problem;
`context` = interpretive reading, not geometry authority;
`watch` = announced work/data without an identified reusable public release.
All entries are unassigned; none is marked implemented merely because it appears
here. R01 is already used by the repository and needs reconciliation, not duplicate
registration. Other ingestion states have not been exhaustively audited.
