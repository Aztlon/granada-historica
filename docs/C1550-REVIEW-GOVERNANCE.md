# c. 1550 review governance

Status: **owner-led internal review**.

The c. 1550 work does not depend on an external historian, university,
museum, public institution, or institutional partner. The project owner is the
historical specialist and may record the historical decision after reviewing
the relevant dossier.

## Meaning of the review roles

The machine-readable `signoffs` are competency and accountability roles, not
organisational affiliations. They identify what was checked, by whom, in what
capacity, on what date, and with what decision. A reviewer does not need to
represent an institution. One person may cover more than one role when they
have the relevant competence, but each decision remains separately recorded so
that historical, architectural, spatial, linguistic, and sensitive social
claims are not silently conflated.

`ready_for_specialist_review` therefore means “ready for an identified person
acting in the relevant review role.” It does not mean “waiting for an external
specialist package or institutional partnership.”

## Current responsibility

- **Historical:** owner-led. Ready for explicit review and approval, revision,
  or rejection by the project historian.
- **Architectural:** a separate decision about building phases and fabric. It
  may be completed internally if that competence is available; otherwise it
  remains pending without blocking research work.
- **Geometry:** a separate audit of spatial evidence and precision. It may be
  performed within the project and does not require institutional affiliation.
- **Translation:** editorial review of the bilingual public text.
- **Morisco history:** a focused historical/ethical check of coercion,
  conversion, revolt, and retrospective projection. It may be assigned to the
  project historian when within their scope, but requires its own recorded
  decision.
- **Population geography:** applies only before publishing derived social or
  demographic areas; it does not block source research or ordinary monuments.

Pending roles prevent public promotion where their subject matter is relevant.
They do not prevent additional research, internal visualization, source review,
or preparation of auditable geometry.

## Institutional collaboration

Institutional collaboration may be pursued later for publication,
stewardship, hosting, or additional peer review. It is optional future work,
not a prerequisite for M10 research or the internal c. 1550 comparison.

No sign-off is inferred from authorship or conversation. The project records an
approval only after the responsible reviewer explicitly gives that decision.
