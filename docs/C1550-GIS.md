# M10.2–M10.5 — Private c. 1550 research GIS package

Status: implemented on 2026-10-01
Publication state: research only; not imported by the application
Working CRS: ETRS89 / UTM zone 30N (EPSG:25830)
Versioned exchange CRS: WGS84 (EPSG:4326)

## Package

The initial review package lives entirely under `data/research/c1550/`:

- `points.geojson`: 25 representative or surviving-site points;
- `lines.geojson`: ten retained or inherited axes;
- `areas.geojson`: four bounded monument or retained-system sites;
- `geometry-audit.json`: one period-specific `in_review` record per geometry;
- `manifest.json`: package counts, rules, included IDs, and explicit deferrals.
- `m10.3-review.json`: the bilingual eight-entity slice, cited claims,
  relationships, geometry decisions, and accountable approval gate.
- `m10.5-geometry-wave-1.json`: the 16 low-risk promotions and the exact 14
  source-reviewed records still kept geometry-free.
- `m10.5-geometry-wave-2.json`: six controlled promotions from those deferrals,
  eight continuing deferrals, and the applicable controls and exclusions.

The initial files were reproducibly assembled by
`scripts/seed-c1550-research.ts`. The script refuses to overwrite an existing
package unless `--force` is passed, so routine research and QGIS edits remain
canonical in the GeoJSON rather than being silently reset to the seed.

All 39 features use `period_id: "c1550"`, a `c1550.*` geometry-variant ID,
separate temporal and spatial confidence, resolvable citations, a geometry
method, geometry sources, and `publication_status: "research"`. No audit is
`verified`; that requires the M10.3 specialist review.

## Phase decisions

The Palace of Charles V is a point, not the current completed footprint. The
Patronato dates the start of work to 1533, the patio foundation to 1540, the
chapel crypt to 1542, and the second storey to 1550. A footprint requires a
georeferenced phase plan.

The Puerta de las Granadas is also a point. Its trace and foundations date to
1545–1548, but the imperial shield is documented in 1552 and completion in the
1590s. The c. 1550 state is therefore explicitly `under_construction`.

The Pilar de Carlos V moved from probable to secure presence after the
Patronato source dated its execution to 1545. The source registry and inventory
record that change.

San Miguel Bajo preserves a particularly useful temporal cut: its first
campaign dates to 1528–1539 and its second to 1551–1556. M10.2 represents the
site as `partially_in_use`, but does not invent a polygon for the first half of
the church. San Cristóbal remains `under_construction` because its enlargement
spans 1540–1559.

The Maristán polygon is retained only as a site envelope. Archaeological
research supports its conversion to the Royal Mint from 1497 and identifies
furnaces, a treasury, and reorganized cells, but M10.2 does not reconstruct a
complete workshop plan for 1550.

## Deliberate deferrals

The manifest blocks six kinds of unsupported precision:

1. phase footprints for buildings still under construction;
2. separate footprints for the attached Cathedral–Royal Chapel–Lonja–Madraza
   complex;
3. Bib-Rambla, Campo del Príncipe, and Plaza Nueva polygons;
4. citywide and population-geography polygons;
5. parish boundaries and the mosque-to-collegiate transition at El Salvador;
6. changed acequia, street, and river-corridor alignments.

Each deferral names the affected entities, the reason, and the evidence needed
to release it. Modern parcels and present-day neighbourhoods are not acceptable
substitutes.

## QGIS workflow

Create the isolated working project with:

```powershell
npm run gis:c1550:bootstrap
```

This creates `gis/work/granada-historica-c1550-research.gpkg` and
`gis/granada-historica-c1550-research.qgz`. Both are local generated files. The
project title and layer group explicitly say `INVESTIGACIÓN — NO PUBLICAR`.

After an intentional edit, update the affected audit note and export with:

```powershell
npm run gis:c1550:export
```

The exporter writes only to `data/research/c1550/`, restores JSON fields,
requires `publication_status: research`, checks all source IDs, and runs the
full validator. Sub-projection numerical noise and reversed polygon ring order
are normalized back to the previous canonical geometry; a material edit is
retained for review.

## M10.3 review slice

M10.3 has assembled the Cathedral-centre and imperial-Alhambra clusters,
completed bilingual period content, and exposed predecessor/successor and
attached-building relationships. The dossier remains
`ready_for_specialist_review`: no audit is promoted until an identified
historical, architectural, geometry, and bilingual reviewer approves it. See
[M10-3-REVIEW.md](M10-3-REVIEW.md).

Those labels describe accountable competencies, not external or institutional
dependencies. Historical review is owner-led, and institutional affiliation is
not required. See
[C1550-REVIEW-GOVERNANCE.md](C1550-REVIEW-GOVERNANCE.md).

The public period selector remains out of scope until M10.4 and the c. 1550
period remains `research` until M10.6.

## M10.5 candidate

M10.5 now uses all 39 geometries in the feature-flagged internal comparison.
The line and areas remain retained-system/site representations, while active
building phases remain points. The candidate explicitly defers public-space,
parish, population, and citywide polygons until their documented derivations
and specialist reviews exist. See [M10-5-CANDIDATE.md](M10-5-CANDIDATE.md).
